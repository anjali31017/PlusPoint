How We Work

At PlusPoint, we deliver news and knowledge in real-time, powered by AI and a community-driven ecosystem. Our platform automatically summarizes content for quick reading and provides personalized recommendations so you see stories that matter most to you. Users can follow firms to receive updates, like and endorse articles, and track their favorite content. Verified users who complete KYC can own firms, publish articles, and access detailed dashboards to monitor trends, engagement, and performance. To maintain quality, all content is evaluated through a trust factor system. Articles from users with lower trust factors undergo moderation before publication, while the trust factor itself is updated hourly based on likes, endorsements, and reports. This ensures a reliable and responsible content experience for everyone on PlusPoint.
