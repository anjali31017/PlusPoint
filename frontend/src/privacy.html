Privacy Policy

PlusPoint respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This section outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and profile details for registration.
  • KYC Verification Data: Government-issued ID and fingerprint for verification and duplicate prevention.
  • Content & Interaction Data: Articles you create, like, endorse, and your following list.
  • Usage Data: AI-based recommendations, dashboards, notifications, and device/browser info.

How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver personalized news feeds and AI-generated summaries.
  • Verify user identity and prevent duplicate accounts via KYC.
  • Enable firm ownership, article creation, and dashboard analytics.
  • Track engagement, send notifications, and maintain the trust factor system.
  • Ensure security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Content & Liability

Users are responsible for the content they create, endorse, or publish. Firm owners are accountable for articles published under their firm. Content from users with low trust factors undergo moderation before publication to maintain quality and reliability.

User Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your account information anytime.
  • KYC and biometric data are used solely for verification purposes.
  • Account deletion requires a formal request; firm or article deletion requires contacting PlusPoint support.
  • Control notifications and manage your following list from account settings.

Data Security & Cookies

  • Industry-standard encryption protects sensitive data in transit and at rest.
  • Regular monitoring prevents unauthorized access or data breaches.
  • Cookies and analytics improve user experience and personalize content without exposing personal information.

Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@pluspoint.com.
